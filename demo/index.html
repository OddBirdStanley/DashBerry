PAGE SCRIPTS (--risky-scripts stand-in)

What dashberry-install --risky-scripts DIR deposited at /usr/local/lib/dashberry/scripts. One name per line, already filtered to executables and sorted, as the panel's own scan leaves them. A 5 s CENTER hold stops both recorders and opens the list; CENTER runs one; the only way out is a reboot. The list has no title row — it is JW-1's shape, four names with the selected one under an INVERTED bar — so a fifth entry is what makes it scroll.
